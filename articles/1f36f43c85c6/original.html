To the ppl of Japan,

I begin with Respect.

Your community has done what too many crypto communities fail to do, it has preserved accountability. It has documented claims, compared statements, flagged harm, and refused to let outrage replace evidence. That discipline matters. In a market built on speed, anonymity, and spectacle, truth survives only when someone keeps the receipts.

I write this as a member of the US caw community, and as someone who chose to examine this matter through the narrow door that truth requires.. wallet trails, transaction hashes, self-published statements, captured public posts, and the exact words used by the operator at the center of this record.

That operator is known as yamasan, @CAWmunityJAPAN, CAW Fun Community Organizer, M. Kuroda, Masashi Kuroda, Kuroda Masashi, 黒田昌資, and kurosan. In his own captured profile and posts, he presents himself as the planner and deployer of a “CAW Support Fun Token Ecosystem,” naming CAWELON, EMA, DAT, DEASP, and CBJPN.

This article is not written as performance. It is written as an evidentiary record. It does not claim that every token lane is closed. It does not pretend that every contract has been recovered. It does not turn allegation into verdict. It does something more useful..

it separates what the record proves from what remains unproven.

What the record already shows is serious enough.

Public sale instructions directed community funds to wallet 0xa754c2ea1f40b27b74d963756bc5cba77f8a8e62, alongside MEXC UID 29489563, as part of the CBJPN sale flow. The same ecosystem was presented through self-attributed authorship, scarcity language, off-chain promises, and a complaint posture that treated buyer disputes as noise rather than accountability. On-chain, the strongest current picture is not a single dramatic rug. It is a controlled market structure.. using belief first, payment rails second, explanations later.

That is why this title is not metaphor.

The question is not whether Yamasan built an ecosystem. He did.

The question is whether that ecosystem functioned as a real market, or as an architecture designed to simulate legitimacy while directing money through rails the public could see more clearly than it could verify.

He did not need one violent move. He only needed a stage, a story, and a system that made arrangement look like trust.

That is the false market examined here.

Standard of Proof

This article proceeds by one rule..

No proof, no authority.

Where the on-chain record is direct and verifiable, I state it plainly. 
Where the record is incomplete, I state that plainly. 
Where the complaint corpus alleges serious harm that is not yet closed by wallet trails, contract mechanics, or transaction-level proof..

I identify it as allegation, not verdict. That is not caution for its own sake. It is the only way to produce a document stronger than the person it examines.

For that reason, the record below is separated into four categories,, 
what is proved from the current file record, 
what is supported but still incomplete, 
what is alleged in complaint materials, 
and what is not yet proved on the present record.

A stronger article is not the one that says the most. It is the one that says only what the evidence can carry.

Exhibit A — Self-Attribution, Self-Branding, and Claimed Control

The first exhibit is not hidden in code or buried in a wallet. It is the operator’s own public self-description.

In the captured @CAWmunityJAPAN profile on X and post archive, the account identifies itself as “Hybrid Creative Planner(HCP),” “BlockChain Backend SE(BBS),” and “M.kuroda.” Most important is the authorship claim published in the profile itself,,

“CAW Support Fun Token Released in Dec 2023 CAWELON・EMA・DAT Plan & Deploy.”

The same archive ties the account to the names Masashi Kuroda, Kuroda Masashi, 黒田昌資, and kurosan.

That matters because attribution often fails at the first step. Here, it does not. The operator does not speak as a distant supporter. He places himself inside authorship.

The captured posts continue that pattern. On March 30, the account publicly presents a 13-dApp ecosystem that includes cbjpn.xyz, deasp.xyz, cawelon.xyz, emadao.xyz, cawsocialclearinghouse.xyz, cawthereum.xyz, aithereumu.xyz, assetdeploy.xyz, hitchhikeguide.xyz, 4269bet.xyz, emabet.xyz, horseracedao.xyz, and horseracebet.xyz, all framed as one CAW support architecture. On March 26, the same account states in substance that it launched #cawelon on March 25, 2024, launched #ema on November 1, 2024, and would launch #DEASP thereafter. On April 3, it states that $DEASP and $CBJPN will be developed simultaneously.

That is not proof of fraud by itself.

It is proof of claimed authorship, claimed continuity, and claimed operational control over the ecosystem story. When an operator publicly brands himself as the planner and deployer of the tokens at issue, that self-attribution becomes the bridge between the narrative presented to the community and the financial rails that follow.

Exhibit B — The Published Payment Rails

The second exhibit is where narrative becomes intake.

The strongest preserved sales-language artifact in the record is the March 13 CBJPN lane described in the local investigation files as a “Secret Whale Sale” naming both,

MEXC UID 29489563 
and wallet 0xa754c2ea1f40b27b74d963756bc5cba77f8a8e62 
as the payment route.

The same preserved lane frames the sale with restricted participation, bonus language, and urgency. It also instructs those who sent funds to report by DM. That is not neutral market infrastructure. That is directed intake.

A separate preserved statement dated March 6 says that all USDT from the CBJPN secret pre-order phase would be identified by sender wallet and consolidated into Camelot DEX liquidity. That matters because it turns the payment rail into a promise.. money in, liquidity later.

Yet the same source set does not provide the one object that would close that promise technically.. a recoverable public CBJPN token contract with a deploy transaction, a public explorer page, and a treasury trace.

This is the first major fracture in the market structure.

Money has a route.
 The promise has a slogan.
 The public contract does not arrive with the same clarity.

That is how a false market begins.. not with the absence of payment, but with the mismatch between payment visibility and asset visibility.

The same attachment set reinforces a second promoted wallet, 0x6510a4b7fada57d793f12fb9e54fe2c3961b6ea5, described there as a wallet used for the DAT pre-registration lane. That second address does not close the case.

It widens the intake pattern.

Exhibit C — The Complaint Posture

The third exhibit is not a wallet. It is a doctrine.

The preserved X-app record captures the operator’s post-sale posture. It states that on March 22 the account framed proof disputes and refund complaints as user ignorance, treated blocking and removal as “security filtering,” and threatened “Digital Execution” by minting screenshots of critics as NFTs. The same record says a March 10 post moved discussion into a Telegram group as a replacement for LINE OpenChat. Another preserved summary states the same posture in substance,, “ArbScan is the one and only proof,” refund or non-receipt complaints are treated as ignorance, and screenshots of critics may be published permanently.

That matters because it reveals how friction was administered. A legitimate operator may dispute a false allegation. A legitimate operator may block harassment. But when the same ecosystem contains private-sale claims, missing delivery complaints, and directed payment rails, language like “noise,” “security filtering,” and “Digital Execution” becomes evidence of how dissent is processed. It ceases to be style. It becomes policy.

The complaint corpus preserved in the file set alleges a broader pattern.. limited or secret presales, celebrity-name and image use, payment requests routed through a wallet and exchange UID, blocking or ghosting, threats against critics, and the continued absence of public contract visibility.

Those allegations are serious. They are part of the record. They remain allegation unless tied to a closed payment and delivery trace.

That distinction is essential, and I keep it.

On the present file record, the following statements are supportable:

The operator publicly self-attributed planning and deployment language for a CAW-adjacent multi-token ecosystem. The preserved off-chain record names MEXC UID 29489563 and wallet 0xa754c2ea1f40b27b74d963756bc5cba77f8a8e62 as the CBJPN sale rail. The strongest courtroom-safe on-chain statement is narrower than “everything is solved,” but stronger than “there is nothing here.” The record supports a claimed multi-token ecosystem, a published intake rail for CBJPN, a promoted BSC wallet that behaves like a routing instrument, and a real CAWELON launch spine with continued funder-family activity. It also supports a complaint posture that treated criticism and refund disputes as disorder rather than accountability.

What the Present Record Does Not Yet Prove

This article should say this plainly, because honesty is not a footnote. It is the spine.

The present record does not yet prove any of the following as closed fact:

  • that 0xa754c2ea1f40b27b74d963756bc5cba77f8a8e62 is conclusively Yamasan’s personal-beneficiary wallet
  • that a public CBJPN contract has been recovered and tied end-to-end to the sale rail
  • that a full victim-payment-to-beneficiary chain has been reproduced for the CBJPN lane
  • that CAWELON has been proved as a classic LP-drain or hard rug
  • that every public complaint against Yamasan is technically solved on-chain
  • that every wallet in the surrounding ecosystem is under common control
  • that the routing-wallet case, by itself, proves beneficiary identity, treasury misuse, or scam intent beyond dispute.

The present file set also preserves explicit stop conditions from the investigation itself: do not promote CAWELON to “hard rug proven” without an abuse path tied to creator, funder, or upstream LP custody; do not promote CBJPN to “on-chain rug proven” without a real victim payment, token contract, or treasury path; and do not merge the Base CAWELON lane with the CBJPN sale lane unless a deterministic bridge address or transaction appears. Those cautions are not weakness. They are evidence of methodological integrity.

Excluded Inferences

  • “CBJPN contract recovery is complete.”

Bottom Line

On the present record, the strongest safe statement is this:

A publicly promoted BSC intake wallet exists. That wallet is real, exchange-touching, and shows reproduced intake-and-sweep behavior. Separately, CAWELON is a real Base token lane with a reproducible creator-funder-deploy-liquidity sequence and later funder-family execution activity. Those facts support a structured operator context and a routing-wallet theory. They do not yet close a full victim-loss or contract-rug case for every named token lane.

A false market is not necessarily a chart that dies in one candle. A false market is a structure in which legitimacy is staged before accountability is supplied. The architecture comes first. The victim often arrives later.

One-Sentence Thesis

The current record proves published intake rails, reproduced routing-wallet behavior, and a real CAWELON launch-and-execution spine; it does not yet prove every named token lane as a fully closed contract rug or victim-loss chain.
