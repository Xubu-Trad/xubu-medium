from humble beginnings to a love I never thought I deserved

by Xubu_Trad

@RyoshiResearch,

Ily, like family.

The kind of love that shows up in hard times, 
stays loyal, 
and means it when it says “I’m here.”

Thank you for what you built, 
and thank you for how you built it!

You did the work and didn’t beg to be worshiped for it.

That alone is rare.

I’m writing this with the kind of care you give to what’s sacred, 
even when you don’t name it out loud.

The kind of care that says love is a verb, 
not a vibe.

The kind of care that keeps your hands honest 
and your mouth humble.

I love the way you taught people 
without preaching at them.

You didn’t demand trust. 
You let the fruit speak. 
You let time tell the truth. 
You let the work stand on its own legs. 
You didn’t chase the best seat. 
You made space for the little ones. 
You didn’t crush the weak to look strong.

I love the way you kept your word simple.

Let your yes be yes. Let your no be no. 
No tricks. 
No performance. 
No selling people dreams while you cash out behind their backs.

That kind of straightness feels like a light in a dark room.

And when the crowd got loud, and people got sharp, and everyone wanted to throw stones, you reminded us to look inward first.

To bless instead of curse. 
To feed people instead of flexing on them. 
To protect the overlooked.

I see the signs you left in the margins.

A lamp isn’t made to be hidden.

Salt has to stay real or it loses its purpose.

A seed can look like nothing and still carry a forest inside it.

A knock matters. A door can open.

The wide road is loud, but it’s not always the right road.

A house only lasts if it’s built on something solid.

So here’s the truth, said plain, I’m grateful for you.

I’m proud of what you put into the world. 
And I love the way you did it without needing to be crowned for it.

If you ever wonder if it mattered, 
hear it from someone who means it it mattered.

it still matters.

and I’m still trying to walk the good way because of it.

With love like family, always,,,

-XT
