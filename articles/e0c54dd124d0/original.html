Dear Shytoshi,

Peace to you in a noisy hour, peace that does not come from winning a crowd, but from speaking cleanly and standing accountable. I write as someone who has walked through enough cycles of promise and collapse to recognize the smell of danger when it’s still dressed as revelation. I have seen “new light” announced with certainty, timed like a spectacle, defended with contempt, and later, when the smoke cleared… what remained was not wisdom but wreckage!.. friendships thinned, trust shattered, the vulnerable embarrassed for believing, and the loudest voices moving on as if nothing happened.

That is why I’m writing now, before more people get scorched by heat that feels holy only because it is intense.

You have pointed to real tremors. Institutions strained, ugly truths surfacing, systems showing their seams, and naming what is shaking can serve a wake-up purpose when it is done with sobriety.

But there is a line you are crossing… you are not merely observing the world, you are baptizing the turbulence into your own narrative and asking people to treat your interpretation as the hinge of history. That is not discernment, that is capture. It takes what is public and verifiable and turns it into a private throne that cannot be tested without being treated as rebellion.

Your own words reveal the spirit at work.

When you speak of a desire to “melt ALL FACES. BAR NONE,” you are not describing a burden to serve… you are describing an appetite to conquer!

When you promise to “unseal” Daniel and “disprove every biblical scholar,” you are not inviting people into truth… you are placing yourself above correction.

When you brand critics as those who “deserve to be ash & ashamed,” you are not guarding holiness, you are teaching people to fear questions…

And when you use phrases like “Bless x-1Million” in the middle of conflict, it does not land as blessing; it lands as a blade with sacred paint..! Then you tighten it with the false choice… “Sit with this or stand with me” … as if conscience must bow to your posture.

This is not the way of light. Light does not demand intimidation. Light does not need fog. Light does not recruit contempt to protect itself.

Hear this plainly,

the first thing that collapses in movements like this is not doctrine… it is love. And when love collapses, truth becomes a weapon in the hands of whoever shouts best.

That is why the test is not how dramatic your “drop” is, or how many people you can impress, or how perfectly you can stage a debate. The test is what your voice produces, does it make people humbler, steadier, more honest, more merciful, more willing to protect the weak and correct themselves… or does it make them harsher, more paranoid, more divided, more eager to shame, more addicted to spectacle?

A message can be clever and still be poisonous.

A message can be loud and still be empty.

So I am calling you to something simple and severe, because anything less will be taken as permission to keep lighting fires.

If you truly believe you are carrying something worth saying, then do it in daylight… write the claim plainly, show the sources, explain the method, and name what would prove you wrong. Remove the countdown pressure. Remove the theatrics. Remove the threats. Stop turning “blessing” into a club. Stop treating questions as enemies. Do not hide behind mystery as if mystery were evidence. If it is truth, it will stand without intimidation. If it is from God, it will not need contempt to defend it!

And if it is meant to help people, it will not leave the anxious more afraid and the weak more exposed.

And if you refuse… if you insist on the path of dominance, staged certainty, and scorched-earth language …

then understand what you are building, an echo chamber that confuses heat for holiness, and loyalty for faith, and noise for fruit.

That road always ends the same way…

the reasonable leave first, then the vulnerable get harvested by the most manipulative voices, and eventually the movement collapses under the weight of its own pride. You may still keep your platform, but you will lose what a platform is for … trust.

I do not write this to entertain your followers or to trade insults with you. I write it because you are shaping a community’s temperature, and temperature determines whether people can think, repent, reconcile, and build, or whether they only burn.

Choose restraint. Choose clarity. Choose mercy. Choose the harder strength.

the kind that can be questioned without exploding, corrected without cursing, and challenged without threatening.

Let your words become a shelter, not a wildfire.

In sober hope that you will turn while turning still costs little,

from a fellow traveler who has seen what spectacle does to souls

- XT
