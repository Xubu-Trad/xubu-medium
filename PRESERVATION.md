# Preservation record

This collection contains 13 articles as readable text and privacy-screened HTML, with 95 image files. Research-folder paths in article bodies are replaced with `[RESEARCH_PATH_REDACTED]`. Account data is excluded.

`articles_manifest.json` records source URLs and checksums of the public text and HTML copies. `media_manifest.json` records image source URLs and retrieval results. The 104 image references have preserved representations: 100 direct downloads and four browser-served WebP substitutes, totaling 95 unique files. The four corresponding PNG requests returned HTTP 429; their exact PNG bytes are unavailable here. Export resolutions may be smaller than original uploads. Linked pages and dynamic embeds are not full offline mirrors.

`SHA256SUMS.txt` covers every published file except itself. Checksums verify copy integrity, not the truth of article claims. See [verification notes](VERIFICATION.md). No new license is imposed on embedded third-party material.
